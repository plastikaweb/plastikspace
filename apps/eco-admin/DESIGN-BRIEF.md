## The prompt

You are designing **Eco Admin**, the internal administration app for a multi-tenant e-commerce platform serving ecological consumer cooperatives in Catalonia. Cooperative operators (volunteers, not tech professionals) use it weekly to manage products, orders, weekly order cycles, members and store configuration. A second role (platform superadmin) manages the cooperatives themselves.

This is **NOT a greenfield design**. A production design system already exists — brand "Organic and Kind": natural, light, welcoming; Material 3; OKLCH-derived palettes; Manrope. Your job is to **extend it with an admin-density layer**, not to invent a new one. Every token below is law: do not introduce new colors, fonts, radii or spacing values. Where you need something the tokens don't provide, derive it from them and flag it explicitly as a proposal.

### 1 · Constraints (hard)

- **Component vocabulary: Angular Material 3.** Every component you draw must map to a Material component (`mat-table`, `mat-chip`, `mat-sidenav`, `mat-card`, `mat-button-toggle`, stepper, datepicker…) or be an explicit composite of them. Name the mapping in annotations.
- **Light AND dark** for every key view. The system uses CSS `light-dark()`; both modes ship day 1. There is also a `prefers-contrast: more` variant — you don't need to design it, but avoid patterns that would break under a higher-contrast palette (no meaning carried by subtle tints alone).
- **WCAG 2.1 AA**: contrast, focus states visible, no color-only status encoding (icon or text label always accompanies). **Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile breakpoints is a house rule, not WCAG AA** — WCAG 2.1's 44×44 (2.5.5) is Level AAA and the AA criterion is WCAG 2.2's 2.5.8 at 24×24; we hold the stricter bar deliberately. **Round 40 ruling (2026-09-25):** on desktop, 2.5.8 is read literally for controls (no control below 24×24, search fields included — the pill is the target); the spacing exception covers inline text links only; the design-system page's own documentation chrome is out of scope. **Estadístiques chart bars (owner, 2026-10-17, 40D answer 7):** the altes bars of Vista Estadístiques are chart marks, focusable and not clickable; they are exempt from the literal 24×24 box and stay focusable — the rule covers controls. In `Vista Lliuraments`, 48 px binds the run's own controls and its identity links (member name, order code) at every width (AORD-09; owner, 2026-10-02, 40B answers 5–6); below 792 the shared components (§19: top bar, drawer, snackbar, close dialog) stay at 44, and from 792 they follow the desktop rule above.
- **UI copy in Catalan.** Section names: Tauler (dashboard), Sol·licituds (requests), Socis (members), Comandes (orders), Cicles (cycles), Productes, Categories, Botiga (store settings), Cooperatives (tenants, superadmin only).
- **Desktop-first** (1280+), functional at tablet; mobile only needs: dashboard, requests inbox, store open/close — plus the delivery run (`Vista Lliuraments`, AORD-09, 48 px targets). **Round 40 ruling (2026-09-25):** those are the only views owed a phone (the shell's CALAIX state, viewport < 792); the login screen joins them (owner's answer of 2026-09-25 to the record's question 5); every other view is owed «functional at tablet» = a content panel of **≥ 720 px** and nothing below; `Vista Socis (mòbil)` is an unmaintained extra outside the contract.
- **Operator copy is copy** (added 2026-08-18 after five review rounds of the same finding; round 30 adds: no comparison symbols in operator strings — «en 14 dies o menys», never «≤ 14 dies»; applies to new or reopened copy, frozen strings elsewhere are not reopened by it): no requirement IDs (`AREQ-07`), HTTP codes (`409`), schema names or implementation terms (`hook`, `Batch API`, `transacció`, `hard delete`, `user → null`) in operator-facing text. Mock-only annotations (open decisions, "pending AQ-10") live exclusively in the SPEC block of the mock, tagged as such. Confirm dialogs and error states must state only what the operator can act on — and never promise a communication (email, notification) that the backend does not send.

### 3 · What to deliver — part A: the admin-density extension spec

The storefront is airy and generous; the admin needs **data density without losing the organic warmth** (warmth lives in: surface tint layering, 16px card radius, pill accents, Manrope, generous section spacing — NOT in bloated row heights). Specify as tokens + component specs:

1. **Density scale**: table row heights (comfortable ≈48px / dense ≈40px — **one app-level user preference stored with the user's prefs, never a per-table control**), form-field height **40px desktop default / 44px on touch breakpoints** (name the `mat-form-field` density level for each; Angular Material steps 4px from 56, so 44 ≈ −3 and 40 ≈ −4 — verify before pinning), list paddings — expressed with the `--space-*` scale. Pin the **height inventory** too: chips 24 (status) / 32 (interactive), buttons 40 / 36 / 32 with their `mat-button` density, and list every M3 deviation (top bar 56 vs 64, rail 72 vs 80, drawer 256 vs 360, chip 24 vs 32) explicitly as density deltas.
2. **Data table pattern** (`mat-table` base): header treatment on `surface-container`, sorted-column affordance, row hover/selected states (use state-layer opacities), sticky first/action columns, inline-edit cell state, pagination bar, bulk-selection bar that appears on selection, empty state, loading state. Hover/selected state layers must propagate to the sticky cells (opaque background, same tint). Name the row-tint tokens generically (`--admin-row-warn-bg` / `--admin-row-error-bg`) — the RGPD escalation and CSV error rows both consume them.
3. **Filter bar pattern**: search input + chips/selects row above tables; collapsed→expanded on tablet. **Round 12 — one chip anatomy and a two-pattern rule:** selected chip = secondary-container fill + leading check, inactive = outlined, live «· N» counts from the same query as the rows; search always **left** (300px), chips after, actions right; a second filter axis sits after a 1px outline-variant separator; chips carry `aria-pressed`. **KPI stat-cards double as filters (DS §18) only when** the set is a bounded pipeline (≤ 7 states) with an operationally meaningful aggregate (count + €) on a page scoped to one container — Comandes only — round 14 overturned Cicles' candidacy (near-singleton states, no per-state aggregate, page spans history: an anti-example); entity lists (Socis, Productes) and the Cicles list always use §05 (or nothing). Sol·licituds' right-hand search was a recorded deviation (frozen 4.1g) — **corrected in round 27** (search left, §05). **Round 27 — sections vs chips:** when a tray's filter axis separates **jobs** — values with different lifecycles, sort keys or row grammars (`ACCOUNT_DELETION`'s statutory clock vs `SIGNUP`'s approval) — it renders as **fixed sections** with sticky counted headers, never as filter chips: filters are retrieval machinery, sections are an order of work. Chips remain for retrieval over homogeneous rows, and the two rules compose (Lliuraments runs chips _inside_ its job-sections). An empty section disappears; an all-empty queue shows its success state. **Amendment 12.1 — reset de filtres:** one M3 text button «Neteja els filtres» (`filter_alt_off`, 32px) at the right end of the bar, rendered **only** when filter state differs from the default; it clears search + chips + second axis + KPI selection and returns pagination to page 1 — never sort, density, column preferences, or the Sol·licituds object tabs and their Pendents/Resolts segment (structure, 4.1g / round 30); on activation focus returns to the search input; the filtered-empty state offers the same reset as its primary action.
4. **Status chip system** — the platform's state machines, each chip = color + icon + Catalan label (never color alone):
   - Cycle: `DRAFT` (neutral) · `OPEN` (success) · `PROCESSING` (info) · `COMPLETED` (primary) · `CLOSED` (neutral-dark)
   - Order: `PENDING` · `CONFIRMED` · `PREPARING` · `READY` · `DELIVERED` · `CANCELLED` · `EXPIRED` (map across warning/info/primary/success/neutral/error). **`PAID` is NOT part of this family** — AQ-07 treats `orders.status = PAID` as legacy and drives payment through `paymentStatus`; defining it in both families produces two identical chips on the same row.
   - Payment: `UNPAID` (warning) · `PAID` (success) · `REFUNDED` (info) · `FAILED` (error) — **visually distinct from the Order family** (outlined variant, leading `€` glyph): both families share an order row and `PENDING` + `UNPAID` must never read as two identical warning chips
   - Membership: `TRIAL` (info) · `ACTIVE` (success) · `INACTIVE` (neutral) · `SUSPENDED` (error)
   - Request: `PENDING` (warning) · `PROCESSED` (success) · `DISMISSED` (neutral) — plus the **derived** resolution labels used in the Resolts segments (not status values, never an enum): `Processada` (success) · `Descartada`/`Descartat` (neutral) · `Executada` (info — `ACCOUNT_DELETION` + `PROCESSED`) · `Respost` (success — `CONTACT` + `PROCESSED`)
   - Allowlist: `PENDING` (info) · `CONSUMED` (success) · `REVOKED` (error)
5. **Deadline indicator**: RGPD countdown for deletion requests (~30-day statutory clock): normal → warning ≥14 days elapsed → critical ≥21 days. Design the escalation (chip/badge + inbox row treatment + dashboard card). **Always present on `ACCOUNT_DELETION` rows** (neutral shows "Queden N dies", never a bare age). **Post-reply lifecycle** (REQUIREMENTS AREQ-09, frozen in design round 4.1g): once a reply is recorded the chip becomes `Bloquejada fins <data>` (neutral) → `Data prevista superada` (warning) → `Llesta per executar` (primary) → `Executada` (info); replied requests leave the dashboard's urgent count.
6. **Destructive-action confirm** dialog pattern: consequence text, typed/checkbox acknowledgment for cascade deletes ("this deletes N products"), pill buttons, error-container accents.
7. **i18n field editor**: pattern for JSON `{ca, es, en}` text fields — language tabs or stacked inputs, showing only the tenant's active languages; include the rich-text variant (aboutUs) and the per-language completeness indicator.
8. **Form layout**: 2-column desktop forms with section cards, sticky save bar with dirty-state, inline validation (Catalan messages).
9. **CSV import stepper**: upload → validated preview (per-row ok/error) → commit report. (Member allowlist bulk import.)
10. **App shell** (round 32: seven real component files under `00 Components/` — Rail · Top bar · Calaix mòbil · Diàleg de tancament · Barra de desar · Paginador · Snackbar — each with a declared props API and its DS §19 decision in its own SPEC; a view mounts them, never redraws them): left `mat-sidenav` (collapsible to icons) + top bar.     **Two containers, one grid contract** (added 2026-09-13, design rounds 36–37; supersedes the «Build:» sentence above). The shell and the panel are measured separately. **Shell** (`matchMedia` on the viewport, re-measured through a `ResizeObserver` on the document root because the design tool's preview fires neither `change` nor `resize` after mount): **Ample ≥976** = rail expanded (256) with a working manual toggle · **Plegat 792 ≤ vw < 976** = rail forced to 72, the «menu» button opens the drawer · **Calaix <792** = no rail, compact top bar, drawer — **only on the four phone surfaces** (owner's answer of 2026-09-26, Round 40.3: the views that import the Rail, Lliuraments aside, stay Plegat below 792; Superadmin keeps its own rail and Login has none). The Rail's query is `not all and (min-width:976px)`, the exact negation of Ample's, so a fractional viewport such as 975.2 px (display scaling) cannot fall between the two; the range syntax `(width < 976px)` is not read by Safari 15.0–16.3. The Rail reports its measured state to the view through `onShell` (`'ample' | 'plegat'`, once on mount and then only on change; a view that binds it later gets it on the next update; without `matchMedia` it says `'ample'`); views take their shell state from it (Paquet 40A-2), never from the Top bar. Derivation: 976 = 720 + 256 and 792 = 720 + 72 — the rail yields width only when the panel would otherwise lose a band; gaining a band by collapsing the rail (viewport 1272–1455) is the user's manual choice, never automatic. Device props (`dispositiu: 'auto' | 'mòbil' | 'escriptori'`, `collapsed`) are overrides, default `auto`. **Panel** (`<main>` is the container: `container-type: inline-size; container-name: panell`, `flex:1; overflow-y:auto`, no padding — the padding lives in the wrapper, so the queried content-box is exactly viewport − rail): the three card bands above, as `@container panell` rules on helmet classes — never inline, because an inline `grid-template-columns` cannot be overridden by a container query. Grid items carry `min-width:0` (kills the automatic minimum that let a `nowrap` table blow a column up); cards declare `min-inline-size:300px` **on the border-box** (`box-sizing:border-box` on the card — every card has `padding:1.25rem`, so without it the real floor is 337.5px and two columns overflow the panel at 720–753: found at 37.2 by browser measurement, fixed at 37C). A column may exist only if every card in it keeps ≥300px; unequal pairs (1.85fr 1fr, 1.5fr 1fr, 1.4fr 1fr) either floor the narrow track (`minmax(300px,1fr)` beside `minmax(0,Xfr)`) or stack at Mitjana — decided per view and written in its SPEC with the reason. The wrapper is `max-width:1600px; margin:0 auto; padding:…` with **no `width:100%` and no `box-sizing`** (1600 is content width; the padding sits outside the maximum). Tables inside grid items sit in their own `overflow-x:auto` wrapper. **Known tolerance:** `<main>` is both the scroll container and the query container, so a classic (non-overlay) vertical scrollbar subtracts its width from the queried box — 15px on macOS «always show», 17px on Windows — and at viewport 976 the panel reads ~705 → Compacta with the rail expanded; the design tool's preview (overlay scrollbars) is exact. Closing it is a build decision: shell thresholds + the platform's bar width; `scrollbar-gutter: stable` alone only makes the deficit constant. **Known floor:** below a 360px panel (397.5 where a padded zone wraps the grid, as in Estadístiques) the 300px floor exceeds the single column and `<main>` scrolls horizontally below ~330 — inherent to 300 + 2rem padding, owned by the mobile-convergence round. `container-type` no longer applies layout containment (css-conditional-5), so fixed overlays and sticky bars inside `<main>` are unaffected (probed on Chromium 152). The six banded views (Tauler, Estadístiques ×2, Sòcia, Comanda, Superadmin) carry `$preview` 1500 so the default artboard (panel 1244) shows Ample with the rail expanded. Canonical: DS §13 + §13-bis, the «Tauler — reflow» demo (`amplada: auto`, measured live), rounds 36–37 (DESIGN-REVIEWS 0.2.107–0.2.113).

Three query containers, one floor. The **panel** (`panell`) decides card grids and page layouts — card grids one column at Compacta and `1fr 1fr` from 720; form-page layouts (form card + side column) `minmax(0,1.4fr) minmax(300px,1fr)` at Mitjana and `1.4fr 1fr` at Ampla, floored rather than stacked because both columns keep ≥300 at 720 and the floored rule equals the frozen layout from panel 808 (Producte) / 799 (Categories).     **Tables: the operating minimum governs, scrolling is the residue** (added 2026-09-24, design round 39 — fourteen tables measured; the DS §04 reference table and the seven view tables rebuilt in 39B, closed in 39C and verified in source at 39.10, 39.12, 39.13 and 39.15; the rule lives in DS §13-ter, clauses 0–12, and this paragraph is its digest). Round 36 promised a choice between «scroll with a visible affordance» and «column priority per band», and measurement dissolved it: priority cannot serve two of the six work tables — those whose rows have no route back to a hidden column —, nor 200 % text zoom (min-content grows with the text, the panel does not), nor the Compacta band. **The affordance is the floor under both branches**, not the alternative to one. **Measurement base (0):** every threshold reads on the table's scroll wrapper, never the panel or the viewport — wrapper = min(panel − 60, 1600) − 8 (the 1440 mock → 1116; the Mitjana floor → 652). **No fabricated floor (1):** a table's `min-width` is the sum of the contracted minimums of the columns it keeps visible, never a round number picked by hand (measured: six of seven wide tables declared one above their own sum — 1,065.68 px of overflow with no content cause). **The kept set P1 (2):** identity, status and the row's primary action, plus the selection column where bulk actions exist; internal order identity > status > action > selection, and only selection may cede, only at a band's floor. **Budget (3):** a column costs its min-content, except selection and actions, which cost max-content because 44 px targets never compress; the visible set is P1 plus the longest prefix of the rank order (`data-pri`) whose sum fits the wrapper — computed at runtime from the same reading as Σ P1, a strict prefix (monotonic: a growing window never hides anything), never a hand-written list or a band. In v1 the user does not choose columns: there is no column selector. **Identity is reshaped, never clipped (4):** capping a composite identity cell does not ellipsize it, it cuts it mid-pill; a cap is licit only after the chips and the secondary meta move to the condensed meta line (§16) and the name is the sole ellipsis carrier, with the corpus clamp (`display:-webkit-box; -webkit-line-clamp:1; -webkit-box-orient:vertical; overflow:hidden; overflow-wrap:anywhere`) because `text-overflow:ellipsis` never clips in an auto-width `td`; the identity column then declares a **contracted floor on its `th`, in rem** (Productes 10rem → 181.00; the reference table 13rem → 226.00), which the Σ P1 recipe reads as its cost — the column's legible minimum, not a table floor. Plain-text identities need no reshape. **Recovery is a precondition (5):** a column may hide only where the row has a canonical route to it — the identity link to the detail (§04) or the inline expansion row (§16) — never the ⋮ menu; where that fails the priority branch does not exist and the table scrolls with the full affordance (Comanda (detall)). **Affordance, always, on both branches (6):** while an edge is clipped, two 24 px fades as absolute children of the card (never inside the scroller, whose containing block would drag them over the frozen block; never `mask-image`, which would fade the sticky cells §04 requires opaque), **neutral and translucent** — `rgb(25 29 22 / .1)` light, `rgb(0 0 0 / .4)` dark — and never the card colour (an opaque fade towards the card paints a band lighter than the tinted row it covers: 8 of 8 measured); a 1 px outline-variant hairline on the sticky block's inner edge; a 100–120 ms opacity transition; `scroll-padding-inline` = the sticky block's width plus the 24 px fade while clipped, **and** an `onFocus` on the scroller that, for a keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`) outside the sticky cells and outside a menu, moves `scrollLeft` exactly what the control still lacks (WCAG 2.4.11 — Chrome does not scroll horizontally to a control already partly visible); the scroll region gets `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"` and an accessible name **only while clipped**; the table itself has a name **always** — a visually hidden `<caption>` (`cdk-visually-hidden` in the build) — and every header `th` carries `scope="col"`. **Hint line with a count (7):** when columns hide without anything scrolling, a line outside the scroller says how many columns are missing and where they are recovered; the table references it with `aria-describedby` only while it exists, and only while the table has rows. **Active sort (8):** the sorted column is forced visible; the real invariant is «no overflow while wrapper ≥ Σ P1 + the forced column's cost», below which the table scrolls with the full affordance; one cannot sort by a hidden column. **Sticky pair (9):** identity left, actions right, only while wrapper ≥ Σ P1 (the reference pair sums 369.25 — more than a 345 wrapper leaves for reading). **Mechanism (10):** hiding is `display:none` bound on the `th` and `td` style, never unmounting (indices, remeasurement and cached costs would all break); sticky blocks are marked `data-sc-stick="l|r"`, not inferred; row height must not depend on the visible set (with classic scrollbars a shrinking table flips its vertical bar and the fit oscillates: 55–61 flips in 3 s on an adversarial variant, zero on the real one); costs are read from the table's DOM and compared against the wrapper's true width minus a classic vertical scrollbar. **Row, focus and motion with sticky blocks (11):** the row's colour belongs to the `tr` (an opaque background) and sticky cells inherit it; 44 px targets carry the focus ring inward (`outline-offset:-2px`); a target is never taller than the row minus its hairline (44 × 44 Còmoda, 44 × 39 Densa) and sits centred; the row entrance animates opacity only (`from{opacity:0}`, delay inside the `animation` shorthand) — a `translateY` on a `tr` with sticky cells leaves 3–6 px of vertical travel inside the horizontal scroller; the selected row has its own hover tone and in-row targets use a translucent state layer (on-surface 8 %), never an opaque grey. **The cost is a contract (12):** at a given width the visible set may not change on paging, filtering, a record's state change or an empty table; a **vocabulary row** right after `</thead>` — `<tbody aria-hidden="true" data-sc-voc>`, one row, one `td` per column, all `display:none` — declares everything a column may ever carry: the single widest member of each closed vocabulary (status, payment, primary action, a chip or glyph inside the identity), the cap of each bounded format (three digits for amounts, days and counts — «Preu» is capped at `000,00 €`), one 44 px box per target where the header lacks one, and per-mode content under the same condition; open text at P1 or rank 1 carries the clause-4 floor instead, so its cost is the floor and not the page's longest name; no fabricated width anywhere (no px width on a `th` or `td` except a target's). A table without a recovery route declares no `data-pri` and never hides — it only scrolls. **Outside the rule, explicitly:** the six Estadístiques chart data views — their four columns always show (Δ and % of total are exactly what the chart cannot), they degrade vertically (no `nowrap`: two-line rows from panel 417 on the Vista, then folded rows) rather than by hiding or scrolling, and overflow only a few px below panel 366 («Vendes per cicle») or 349 (the other first and second tables); what fails there is their container, which goes to Round 40. **First build experiment (D.39):** whether hiding columns with CSS invalidates `mat-table`'s sticky offsets. Canonical: DS §13-ter (the rule) and §04 (the reference table); round 39, DESIGN-REVIEWS 0.2.117–0.2.133.

    **Operating modes & module-conditional nav** (added 2026-08-29, design round 22). Three no-cycle states stay distinct and must never be conflated: «Cap cicle actiu» (window tenant between cycles, transient) · «Venda contínua» (continuous, structural) · «Sense cicles / Crea el primer» (window tenant, none created yet).     **Chart data views** (added 2026-08-29, design round 24). Every chart ships a data view (§3.11 a11y rule), but a table earns its place only by carrying what the chart cannot. Three rules: the toggle **swaps** the view, it never appends a table under a chart that already labels its bars — coexistence is what makes the table read as a transcription; the data view is a real `table` (caption, `th scope="col"`, right-aligned tabular figures) and not a list of rows; and it must add at least one column the chart lacks — the comparison against the previous period, the share of the total, or the running total — plus a total row. Zero-value periods, invisible as bars, are the data view's other reason to exist and are always listed («0 altes»).

    **Save bar — one primary at a time** (added 2026-08-26, design round 24; extends §3.16). When a route form also carries a workflow transition (order preparation being the case that forced this), the sticky bar shows exactly one primary: while dirty it is «Desa els canvis» (with Descarta), and only once clean does it become the transition CTA («Passa a "A punt"»).     **The order's two work surfaces** (added 2026-08-29, design round 25). An order is worked twice, by different people in different places, and the two moments need different surfaces. **Preparation** (desktop, at the table): the basket is the work, every line resolves to one of three visible outcomes — accepted as ordered (one tap), adjusted (editing the final quantity marks it reviewed), cancelled as unavailable (struck, out of the total, with an offered reason: preset chips plus free text, optional but pre-offered so giving one is nearly free). A header action resolves every untouched line at once, because a basket with nothing wrong must cost one tap. The transition is **gated on completeness** — «N de M productes revisats», CTA disabled until N = M — and once the order leaves preparation the controls vanish, the basket becomes a read-only summary of what will be charged (cancelled lines still visible with their reason, delta pinned) and the bar's primary becomes the next transition. **Delivery** (phone, at the door or the pickup point): not the order detail at all, but a day's run — grouped by delivery method, each row only what the courier needs, and two **independent** primary actions, «Lliurada» and «Pagada», because payment may happen before, during or after handover and neither may gate the other. Round 26 hardened the run's grammar: **state lives in chips, actions in buttons — never both in one control**; a finished row goes quiet (compact, success-tinted, sunk to its group's bottom) so the remaining work stays loud; the run takes the §05 filter bar (search + state/method chips, the header counters double as filters) and §15 load-more on both shells — a work queue never paginates pending work out of sight. **Status names are one per state across both apps** (eco-admin and the member storefront): a state that reads «A punt» to the admin and «Preparada» to the member is one fact with two names — perspective belongs in a sentence on the member's card, never in a different chip label.

### 5 · Output format

- Anti-goals: no new fonts/colors/radii; no generic "SaaS admin" grey-blue look — this must feel like the same organic brand, denser; no glassmorphism (that's the storefront header's trick, don't copy it into the admin shell).
